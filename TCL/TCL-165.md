# Relationship replacement can delete task-body text and mishandle valid YAML indentation

Independent review of TCL-164 found three pre-existing boundary bugs in replaceArrayField. When related is absent from the frontmatter, applying related: [FOO-2] deletes a body line beginning with related: because the removal regex searches the whole Markdown file. Valid column-0 and four-space block-list items also disagree with the line-based guard and the two-space removal regex, leaving old items behind or failing at updated_at. Reproduce through applyFieldUpdates with a body containing related: see the design doc followed by keep me; the first body line disappears. Reproduce the indentation variants with related followed by - FOO-1 at column 0, or by four spaces and - FOO-1. Fix the shared cause: use YAML parser-owned ranges bounded to frontmatter for relationship removal, rather than a separate regex scan. Preserve body bytes and unrelated frontmatter fields. Keep unsupported anchors, aliases, mappings, and block scalars fail-loud. Read-only reviewer: sandpiper-agent, 2026-10-06. These bugs were not introduced by TCL-164.

## Implemented and reviewed 2026-10-06

Commits ovxloxss and xlozwruu use parser-owned, frontmatter-bounded ranges for relationship removal. No whole-body regex scan remains for array removal. Valid zero-column and four-space list layouts update correctly. Real CLI reproduction preserves body text beginning with related:. Malformed YAML produces its actual parser diagnostic. Duplicate relationship fields refuse explicitly instead of reporting an ineffective update.

Read-only independent review by sandpiper-agent passed span bounds, comments, blank lines, and last-field removal; its duplicate-key finding was fixed with a red/green regression. Full suite after follow-up: 511 tests, typecheck clean. NEEDS REVIEW.
