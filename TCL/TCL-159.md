# Migrate Trail docs out of sandpiper-ai into the standalone repo

Trail's operator and reference documentation still lives in sandpiper-ai (`.sandpiper/docs/`, `packages/resources/skills/sandpiper/tasks/references/`). Standalone Trail carries `docs/` in its published `files` list but the content has not moved.

Scope: identify Trail-owned docs, move them into `~/git/trail/docs/`, and leave whatever sandpiper-ai genuinely needs as its own thin integration doc rather than a copy. TCL-109 was closed WONTFIX specifically because keeping two copies in sync was not worth it — do not recreate that problem in the other direction.

Watch for docs that describe the pre-extraction world: `.sandpiper/tasks` paths, `sandpiper-tasks` command names, and the config shape before TCL-148/149. Moving a stale doc just relocates the staleness.

Deferred out of TCL-146; filed 2026-08-12 when that ticket reached review.

## Ruling on the extraction design record (2026-08-12)

`.sandpiper/docs/specs/trail-extraction-design.md` **splits — it does not move whole, and not yet.**

It self-declares as "explanation + normative decisions," which is the mixed-mode tell. It is serving two audiences with different lifetimes:

| Content | Mode | Audience | Destination |
|---|---|---|---|
| Config resolution (3 candidates), config content (tasks-dir key, no fallbacks, no escape hatch), versioning (exact match), `initialize` semantics, branding rule | normative **reference**, with rationale as **explanation** | anyone using or porting Trail | harvest into `~/git/trail/docs/` |
| The coupling finding (16 path literals), why extract at all, the dependency-consumption ranking (vendor / git dep / `file:` / submodule), extraction sequencing, prior-work assessment | **explanation** | sandpiper-ai maintainers | stays here |
| Migration tool spec | **reference** | sandpiper-ai | stays here — the tool lives here by settled decision |

The cut is clean because the two halves share no content: Trail's half governs Trail's runtime behavior forever, sandpiper-ai's half is the record of one event. **Do not duplicate across the boundary** — TCL-109 was closed WONTFIX precisely because keeping two copies in sync was not worth it. The sandpiper-ai half links to Trail's; Trail's references nothing here.

**Timing gate: harvest only after TCL-161, TCL-162 and TCL-163 land.** Three of the settled decisions are not implemented. Publishing them into Trail's `docs/` today would ship documentation describing behavior Trail does not have — and Trail's `package.json` already includes `docs/` in its `files` list, so that lie would be published.

When harvested, the content changes mode. `[settled]` markers are meaningful during a design session; in Trail's docs this is simply how Trail works, with the rationale kept as explanation. Do not carry the decision-log framing across.

After the extraction completes and the harvest is done, what remains here is historical: move it to `.sandpiper/archive/`.

**Unrelated to placement and more urgent than it:** the record is uncommitted, living in exactly one working copy, while being the authority for TCL-146, 157, 161, 162 and 163. One `rm` and the settled design is gone.
